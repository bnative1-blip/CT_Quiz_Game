    Five questions on this module. Here is exactly how the round runs.

    1. 1

      Wrong answers cost you hardware

      Every wrong answer shears a component off Unit CT-1 beside you. Four wrong and you are on a Performance Improvement Plan. Five wrong and, well — don't.

    Keyboard: A–D or 1–4 to answer, Enter to advance.
